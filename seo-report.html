Your next SEO move

  • First action: Unify Next.js trailing slash routing rules across all subdomains to stop canonical bleeding (79 canonical and duplicate page warnings).
  • Next action: Deepen internal cross-linking to your technical blog posts and sub-projects to resolve the 14 orphan page warnings.
  • What's working: Your new technical blogs (e.g. Distroless Docker Go Apps) are already generating top-10 impressions in Google Search Console, and your AEO JSON-LD architecture is fully active.

Recommendations

Fix Trailing Slash Duplication Site-Wide

Do this

  • Enforce a strict trailingSlash: false in your next.config.ts for the Blog, Portfolio, Web, and Project Sites.
  • Implement a middleware or edge redirect that forces any URL ending in / to 301 redirect to its non-slash counterpart.

Why

  • The crawler found 21 canonicalized versions and 8 duplicate titles/descriptions. For example, /blog, /blog/, and /blog/blog/ are all serving 200 OK responses simultaneously.
  • Googlebot will waste crawl budget indexing duplicate pages, and search equity (link juice) splits across both variants.
  • Expected Effect: Consolidating URLs immediately fixes 37+ technical SEO errors and focuses your domain's ranking power on single canonical paths.

Bridge Orphan Pages

Do this

  • Audit your sub-project /docs and /architecture subpages to ensure they are linked from the main project heroes or footers.
  • Add a "Related Posts" component to your new blogs so they interlink with each other.

Why

  • The audit flagged 14 Orphan Pages. These are pages that exist on the server and in the sitemap but have zero internal links pointing to them.
  • If a page has no internal links, crawlers assume it is unimportant, and users cannot organically discover it without knowing the URL.
